# Purpose accountability

Use this reference for every part, recipe, model, scene, material-role, light, or motion creation and review task.

## Invariant

No authored decision may exist only because it looks interesting, fills space, matches a genre convention, increases detail, or makes a metric move.

Every visible choice must trace through one need chain:

```text
consumer, interaction, or scene need
  -> artifact role
  -> feature or relationship
  -> authored step or generative rule
  -> emitted visible output
```

Every leaf needs a named beneficiary upstream. If nothing elsewhere needs it, remove it.

Never reason backward from existing or generated geometry to an invented need. The need must come from the brief, relationship graph, authoritative state, or demonstrated structural or readability requirement; a post-hoc story is counterfeit until the evidence proves it.

A purpose statement is a hypothesis, not proof. Promotion fails closed until the live artifact and its evidence support the claimed job.

## Choose the accountable unit

Audit authored decisions, not millions of mechanically repeated cells one by one.

- Give every direct voxel operation, part invocation, nested recipe, subtractive void, material-role transition, unique accent, placement, light, and motion its own record or an exact membership in a named feature record.
- Let repeated output share one record only when one deterministic rule gives every member the same job and explicit bounds. Record exceptions separately.
- Give a reusable part a definition-level purpose and each invocation a context-level need. Reuse proves repetition, not necessity at a particular location.
- Treat negative space as authored output when its size, position, or shape is deliberate.
- Split a record whenever one subset can be removed or moved without causing the recorded consequence.

For example, a wall field may share one enclosure record when one bounded fill rule creates it, but a notch, trim band, isolated cap, color stripe, and asymmetric outlier each need their own reason.

## Required purpose record

Record these fields before promotion:

```text
id:
artifact and exact authored scope:
required by:
job performed:
location or relationship datum:
failure when removed:
failure when relocated:
why this is the smallest adequate form:
evidence:
honesty boundary:
```

The record must answer:

1. Who or what needs this?
2. What observable job does it perform?
3. Why must it be here, at this size, orientation, material role, or phase?
4. What specifically stops working or becomes unreadable if it is removed?
5. What specifically stops working or becomes misleading if it moves elsewhere?
6. Could fewer voxels, a simpler part, an existing model, or no element satisfy the same need?
7. Which live geometry, relationship, consumer state, test, trace, or fixed view proves the answer?
8. Which tempting interpretation does the evidence not support?

## Reject counterfeit purposes

These are not sufficient:

- “looks cool,” “adds visual interest,” “breaks up the surface,” “adds detail,” or “balances the composition” without naming what must be read or used;
- symmetry, asymmetry, realism, genre convention, or reference imitation without a dependent need;
- a generic note that could be pasted onto another feature unchanged;
- a fictional load, pipe, power source, joint, control, doorway, route, or interaction that the geometry or authoritative state does not support;
- a placement whose claimed relationship survives arbitrary relocation;
- a feature whose removal has no observable consequence at the intended camera, scale, state, or interaction;
- a large generated cluster justified only by one record when individual exceptions do different work.

Readability is valid only when it communicates a named state, boundary, affordance, hierarchy, orientation, material transition, hazard, route, or relationship to the intended viewer. Appearance without communicated meaning is not a fallback purpose.

## Run the gate

1. Write the artifact job and relationship graph before geometry.
2. Create the purpose records beside the authoring source or in a creator-local registry; do not invent a public schema only to store review evidence.
3. Enforce exact coverage. Match every authored decision or bounded generative group to one record, reject missing or duplicate identities, and reject records that point to absent geometry.
4. Run subtraction. Hide or remove each recorded scope at the intended view and state. If the claimed failure does not occur, delete the scope or rewrite the design and evidence.
5. Run relocation. Move the scope away from its datum. If the job remains equally valid, the original location was arbitrary; re-anchor or remove it.
6. Run simplification. Reduce dimensions, repetitions, colors, joints, or motion until the next reduction breaks the recorded job.
7. Inspect the default camera and an adversarial camera or state. Source prose and structural tests cannot prove visual communication by themselves.
8. For mechanisms, trace ground or parent support, attachment, actuation or transmitted power, allowed degrees of freedom, clearance, contact, transfer, and release. State every unsimulated boundary.
9. Ask a fresh reviewer to infer jobs from the artifact without seeing the purpose ledger. Repair the artifact when the ledger must explain away visible contradictions.
10. Block promotion while any orphan, counterfeit purpose, unsupported relationship, or unproved removal consequence remains.

## Evidence by artifact level

- **Part:** exact callers, shared grammar, parameter bounds, and proof that a second real use needs the same shape logic.
- **Recipe:** exact step-to-record coverage, Build-stage subtraction, occupied and empty-space checks, and material roles tied to named communication or structure.
- **Model:** feature dependency graph, intended-scale multi-view proof, picking or physical parity where relevant, and evidence that simpler alternatives lose the stated job.
- **Scene:** placement relationship graph, coordinate and orientation datums, route and clearance checks, default plus adversarial views, and authoritative evidence for any claimed interaction.
- **Motion:** named state or process communicated at each phase, attachment and handoff truth, pause-state readability, and causal or deterministic evidence proportionate to the claim.

Tests can prove coverage, identity, geometry, relationships, and state. They cannot prove that a result reads correctly. Screenshots can prove visible communication. They cannot prove hidden structure or causality. Use both when the claim crosses both surfaces.
