# Devlog — 2026-08-31

## A target organ is not current tissue

The owner reported the visible consequence directly: “Anything that grows should grow slowly, on a cellular level. You can't just teleport in a bunch of leaves.” The fixture conserved resources and produced deliberate endpoint silhouettes, but a flush date could make most of a target-shaped leaf appear in one accepted frame, and later whole-shape resampling could replace earlier local display cells. Neither an endpoint milestone nor a ledger residual asks whether the path between them is a growth process.

Each active primary-development plan now records stable target length, radius and resource pools, plus target area for a leaf, separately from the current paid values exposed by the immutable snapshot. Target values describe the intended mature plan and do not grant material. Flush authoring pays only a `0.005` primordium fraction. Every `900 s` allocation step can advance at most `0.0025` development fraction and only up to the current schedule cap; the carbon budget and available carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water scale the request before any pool or geometry changes. Paid carbon accumulates in preformed, dividing, expanding, maturing and mature cohorts, whose sum is checked against current structural carbon.

The geometry law follows the paid pool law rather than a wall clock. A structural axis uses one development fraction for both current length and current radius, so its target-pool and planned-volume fraction is `f³`; a temporary `0.5 mm` dimensioned-wood minimum keeps the earliest paid primordium geometrically defined without granting target radius or pools. A leaf uses `f` for current area and `√f` for length and radius, while a bud uses `∛f`. Default-speed and ten-times playback reach identical integrated fractions and pools because both execute the same biological allocation steps.

A created identity is not automatically a placed organ. The prior terminal bud first spends four fixture days swelling and is consumed at bud break; the children it guarded remain authoritative `preformed` records. Exposure then walks the parent chain after allometry. A lateral leaf needs the parent to clear `max(2 mm, 1.5 × current parent radius)`, while a distal axis, branch or bud needs `max(4 mm, 2.5 × current parent radius)`. This admits the leaf at a real node before the next internode has room to emerge. A preformed identity is excluded from placed-shape conflict, mechanics, atmospheric leaf exchange and projection, so hidden target geometry cannot collide or render.

The adapter no longer rasterizes a differently scaled whole shape at every size. It builds a deterministic mature candidate mask from the target dimensions and reveals a connected prefix keyed in organ-local lattice coordinates. Structural candidates are ordered proximal-to-distal with centre-out radial sections and are bounded by the lesser of development fraction and `(current length / target length) × (current radius / target radius)²`. Leaves expose only their current axial extent, commit petiole and midrib before lamina and add lamina proximal-to-distal and centre-out under the lesser of current-to-target area and development fractions. Buds use the same axial/centre-out order. Each wood unit is now one constant circular section from base to tip, so an unfunded child contributes no source cells and cannot alter its mature parent's cylindrical mask. Once emerged, every child base must fit within the parent terminal radius; the retired `0.72` within-unit taper is retained as the counter-control that would produce an outward step.

The distinction between organ-local and world-lattice identity remains important. In still air, an organ's previously committed source cells remain a subset of every later source mask; after fusion, mechanics or a growing parent may move public world-lattice material membership because authoritative pose changes continuously across the display lattice. The retired whole-shape resampling counter-run is kept because it loses old local cells between two increasing leaf fractions, proving that count growth alone is not continuity.

The lifecycle correction exposed another endpoint shortcut. Day 240 now detaches each leaf from its parent, removes it from attached-canopy exchange and diagnostics, and starts a `1.5 day` authoritative fall while retaining identity and carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water pools. Each leaf remains one independent connected rigid voxel body, and breeze travel changes its authoritative endpoint. Day 241 shows the fall. The body reaches a nonpenetrating supported pose and remains visible there for one full host tick; on the next tick soil contact transfers the pools, zeros the organ and marks it abscised while litter receives the exact same cell suffixes, matrices and RGBA values. There is no flattening, terrace packing or cutaway repacking at handoff. The retained browser milestones are now day 14 primordia, visible flushes at days 20, 54 and 100, first retained all-mature day 180, senescence at days 210, 220 and 239, detachment at day 240, falling at day 241 and post-contact litter at day 242. Original-resolution review caught a material discontinuity hidden by the lifecycle assertions: the russet day-239 canopy snapped back to yellow-green on detachment because the phenology palette applied only to `senescing`, not `detached`, leaves. Detached leaves now remain on that same elapsed-season palette, and the day-240 mean colour must retain `R > G > B` with an RGB distance below `15` from day 239.

The biological provenance is deliberately narrower than the implementation. [Pérez-de-Lis et al.](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2435.12789) report `26–40 day` leaf expansion in the studied mature *Quercus robur* trees; the fixture's 28-day schedule window is only a hypothesis and resource limits can extend completion. [Herrmann et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4765786/) support the ordering bud rest → bud swelling → shoot elongation → leaf expansion, not the fixture's exact offsets. [Harmer](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1469-8137.1990.tb00917.x) reports approximately constant *Q. robur* taproot elongation through stem growth and leaf expansion, which supports continuous root extension but not the aggregate cohort or its rates. [Kalve et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/eru358) quantify spatially and temporally distinct cell division and expansion in *Arabidopsis* leaves; that supports separating phases but does not calibrate oak cells. The `131 / 65,536 m` tissue voxel, about `1.999 mm`, is therefore one display sample of a population of tissue cells, not a literal cell, layer, anatomical volume or biomass parcel.

The whole-class evidence now spans three boundaries. Core gates require funded primordia, positive sub-one-percent consecutive advances, cadence-invariant integrated payment, paid cohort conservation, self-similar structural `f³` volume, physical exposure order and retained falling pools to contact. Projection gates require connected nested source prefixes through all three bud breaks, every earlier selected source key to remain represented by material, no preformed source cells, bounded final-lattice membership churn, no parent-cylinder change at child birth, child radii no larger than their parent terminal sections and a failing whole-shape-resampling control. Fall continuity requires every detached mask to remain one connected body, reach support, and hand identical matrices and colours to litter on the next tick. The browser gate selects an ordinary default-speed growth witness and requires both bounded voxel change and a different resulting canvas. These checks prove continuity at the fixture's organ, population-cohort and display-sample scales; they do not claim a cell-resolved oak.

## A finite first-year topology needs a named carbon boundary

The original finite three-flush slice kept assimilating after the final primary-development front completed, but it had no secondary-growth or storage-organ sink. Letting mobile carbon grow without bound would make the ledger close while the biological state became meaningless; labelling the excess respiration would instead falsify the process that removed it.

The fixture now waits until every funded primary sink is complete and the phenology latch is `leaf-mature` or `senescence`. It then exports only mobile carbon above the declared reserve through `post-primary-carbon-overflow`, books that amount in the carbon sink ledger and exposes a separate cumulative diagnostic. A `no-post-primary-carbon-overflow` counter-run is identical through day 90 while growth remains active, then stockpiles the excess after completion. From day 110 to day 200 the baseline and counter-run retain the same organ identities, parents, dimensions, structural pools, source keys and material keys, while only the baseline keeps mobile carbon bounded. This is an explicit first-year truncation boundary, not respiration, cambial growth, a storage organ or a recurrent annual cycle.

## A target rate is not a measured frame rate

The owner reported that rendering felt slow and asked for FPS in the information panel. The host already knew the repository's fixed `60 Hz` simulation step, cumulative runtime frame count, presented revision and GPU resource counts, but none of those values answers how frequently recent canvas frames arrived. Reporting any of them as FPS would have produced a stable-looking number precisely when the display loop was struggling.

The new above-fold `Render frame rate` readout is presentation-only telemetry. Its sampler observes every foreground RAF-driven `runtime.frame` attempt and counts the interval only when that call returns a presented manifest, so repeated unavailable frames drive the recent value to zero while manual command, resize, camera and deterministic harness renders cannot inflate it. Biological pause does not stop measurement because the canvas continues rendering; simulation time and presentation cadence remain separate.

The instrument retains about one second of successful frame timestamps, waits `500 ms` before its first value and updates at most every `250 ms`. It preserves the interval crossing the window boundary, which lets a missed or visibly stalled frame reduce the number instead of disappearing during window eviction. A visibility transition clears the sample history, so browser background throttling is not presented as foreground performance, and disposal removes that listener.

Deterministic controls report `30`, `60`, `120` and `240 Hz`, lower the value for a missed frame, reach zero across repeated unavailable attempts, recover, converge after old slow samples leave the recent window, reject an exact duplicate and reversed time and restart after visibility reset. A host-glue control proves a manual frame produces no observation while an undefined RAF runtime result remains a failed attempt. The browser then starts a deterministic `30 Hz` RAF timestamp stream, pauses the still-`60 Hz` biological host, switches the stream to `20 Hz` and must converge to exactly `20.0 FPS`; this rejects both the simulation target and a stale pre-pause value, while the ordinary machine sets no minimum because load, browser scheduling and display refresh are not product correctness. The value is successful canvas-frame cadence, not GPU completion, compositor scanout or a claim that the known day-100 projection cost already meets the `16.67 ms` target.

The first complete browser gate rejected the ordered wind-start baseline by three percent even though the tree and airflow pixels remained aligned: that canvas capture includes the overlaid HUD, whose new row shifted every following control. The four HUD-bearing wind baselines were regenerated, and only the volatile FPS value is masked in both baselines and motion-analysis frames. The label and row geometry remain visible evidence, while changing digits cannot satisfy a weather or organ-motion threshold.

The owner rejected the gap between numerical authority and what the case study actually showed: “I see no animation for rain or wind, and the soil looks completely flat and therefore fake.” The repair keeps the simulation authoritative, but gives each environmental claim bounded, inspectable voxel evidence.

## Moving weather exposed canonical chunk-index lineage

The root-cutaway rain sequence produced a valid state order the previous still scene had not exercised: a displayed chunk, an accepted chunk change still waiting on a worker, repeated sparse weather-batch targets that superseded presentation, and then another soil chunk change. Canonical state accepted the sequence, but the atomic pipeline constructed the final chunk delta from the last *presented* chunk index even though the delta's base was the last canonically committed candidate, which need not have reached either a worker or the canvas.

The pipeline now records only the lightweight `{candidate, index}` of the last canonically committed target when the runtime crosses its activation boundary. That boundary is after canonical commit, so the lineage is retained even if scheduler revalidation then rejects or blocks target activation; accepted state stands and the next frame must derive from that accepted predecessor. This does not authorize an unpresented mesh for reuse: reusable meshes still come only from the stager's displayed bundle, so each stale displayed chunk in the regression sequence schedules one fresh worker job instead of retaining the prior plan's groups, jobs and batch pages.

The first counter-run presents the initial chunk, commits an unpresented chunk update, supersedes it with two batch-only patches and requires a second chunk update to reserve normally with one mesh job. Full snapshots now pass their explicit canonical base into the same selection: removing and recreating one chunk advances its slot generation through the accepted predecessor instead of resetting an ABA key, while a changed profile in a new epoch starts at fresh generation one and a same-epoch profile mutation remains illegal. Candidate and index lineage is scoped to one world and epoch.

A reviewer then found that recovery treated the existence of *any* active target as success, so a rejected revision-three activation could leave a mismatched revision-two target active and idle forever. Recovery now accepts an active target only when world, epoch and revision all match the pending canonical candidate. Scheduler drift that makes every replacement stale is retried at the existing bound and fails on attempt eight with the actual `reservation rejected: stale-target` outcome rather than reporting only the outer reservation shape.

Two reentrancy windows were separate defects. Coordinator admission is now fenced for every presentation-lease state after `prepared` until settlement clears the current record, including callbacks that reenter after a lease is already `committed` or `aborted`. Scene-root detach and attach themselves emit synchronous `removed` and `added` callbacks, so the lease now enters `swapping` before scheduler commit and root mounting begin; reentrant acceptance from either callback receives `presentation-in-flight`, and the incoming frame remains the one that commits.

Those same synchronous root callbacks exposed two different owner actions that an admission fence cannot solve. A callback that calls `runtime.dispose()` now records deferred disposal until the outer atomic frame aborts or settles and its operation unwinds, then drains teardown synchronously with the root empty, the runtime disposed without a failure and a repeated dispose as a no-op. The standalone frame returns quietly. Embedded-host preparation detects the ended generation after activation, aborts and settles that target, and rejects with the existing `three.frame-ticket.late` code before issuing a ticket; commit uses the same existing code after consuming its ticket; abort completes its rollback before teardown. A callback that calls standalone `runtime.frame()` instead receives a clear error before any nested draw, so a transient empty or incoming scene cannot be stamped as the prior presented revision without widening an exported protocol-code union.

The first implementation did widen both exported result unions, and the public-API snapshot rejected it. The final contract retains the existing unavailable reasons and protocol codes; `api/public-api.json` changes only because the generated class declaration records the compatible private disposal-owner extraction.
