# 2026-09-01

## Supporting planes do not prove a material hinge

The attachment test originally called separate axial and radial support equalities exact node-envelope tangency. Independent review produced a counterexample with `organ:2:1`, `0.0015 m²` area, `0.075 m` length, `0.3 rad` roll, parent `+Y` and leaf/radial `+X`: the basal half-width and half-thickness are `0.913733744514 mm` and `0.391600176220 mm`, but the fixed node datum has local thickness coordinate `-0.615367314993 mm`. It lies outside that section by `0.223767138773 mm` even though both plane tests pass. The gate now preserves that numerical counterexample and the docs call the model a fixed kinematic datum with conservative two-plane clearance. Geometry did not change; exact material contact remains unresolved. The fracture check now also observes the ordinary controller immediately before and at day 240, while the litter offset check separates the exact source-space `2 mm` displacement from the later terrain-support translation.

## A short straight run does not prove a terrain component is local

The first five-level relief gate measured populations, cliffs, isolated columns and straight runs, yet its `-2` level still contained one `540`-cell four-neighbour component spanning all `40` columns and all `40` rows. The hero view exposed the result as giant stepped mesas. The replacement mixes seven low-frequency directions before quantization and retains the exact collar and five-to-one soil/tissue alignment while splitting the surface into `18` contour bodies. The largest body is `212` cells, its longest axis span is `23`, and the largest exact pairwise Euclidean diameter is `26.8701` cells; bounds of `220`, `24` and `27` now gate those three properties independently, and the superseded surface is the counter-run that fails area first. The eight process-soil cells remain authoritative, while root carving, cutaway, rain contact and litter support continue to consume the shared disposable surface oracle.

## A connected leaf prefix must not destabilize structural material

The first incremental-growth repair still sent every changing leaf source through the shared world-lattice allocator. A leaf could grow by one organ-local display sample while unrelated branch ownership, repair paths and public keys changed around it; an attached collar and later scar could also affect structural routing. Endpoint connectivity and a single day-100 churn ratio did not distinguish a local growth front from global allocator work.

The canonical material allocator now owns only wood, roots, seed and buds. Each attached or falling leaf remains one connected organ-local voxel-block body: lamina samples are equal cubes, while its complete basal petiole and midrib use tapered anisotropic cuboids at the same axial sampling pitch. The authoritative attachment uses a fixed kinematic node datum and independent axial/radial section-support offsets; it does not prove material contact with the conservative parent-node envelope. Only disposable projection adds the `2 mm` source offset and publishes a parent-owned topological witness; terrain-supported litter preserves its horizontal part and accounts separately for its vertical correction; the fixed milestones and complete breeze cycle measure a `4.791602945 mm` maximum public separation under the `4.79161 mm` cap, whose rounded `7.055 nm` Float32 margin does not imply face contact. At base abscission, the same already-existing parent-union record selected by that topology is recolored as the zero-mass wound; no collar or scar block is allocated beside the union. Three 180-tick windows after days 20, 54 and 100 separately gate bounded structural membership changes, monotonically retained leaf source prefixes and at most four new display samples in one leaf body per host tick. Public accepted-frame checks consume the actual Float32 affine matrices and reject cross-body positive-volume overlap across growth, senescence, staggered fall and a complete breeze cycle; the separate one-micrometre physical fracture cleft begins only after detachment and prevents serialization from turning the intended wound/leaf separation into overlap without making a visible jump.

## An abscission scar is a wound, not one percent of a leaf

The first detachment bookkeeping copied an arbitrary fraction of all four whole-leaf pools into the parent when it created a short visible scar. That closed the ledger but gave a material wound an unsupported biological mass transfer. Leaves now linearly resorb `50%` of starting structural nitrogen and `52%` of starting phosphorus into the plant mobile reserve from day 210 to each leaf's own staggered fracture; each denominator includes that leaf's `0.8 day` stagger rather than letting later leaves hit the target early and wait. Base abscission keeps the complete petiole and all post-resorption carbon, water and nutrients with the falling leaf until soil contact. The wound is only a recolor of one existing nearest parent surface cube. Detachments start at day 240 and stagger every `0.8 day`; each fall lasts `1.5 day`, so the retained browser states are first detachment at day 240, mixed attached/falling/litter at day 244 and complete litter at day 249.

The scar topology key originally omitted `leaf.identity.localId` even though that identity was embedded in separately allocated public scar source keys. Base abscission removed those extra keys: wound records now reference already-present canonical parent cells, while identity and wound-location mutations still force conservative topology rebuilds with exact cached/cold parity. A counter-run that appends wound records disagrees with the published wood count.

## A cutaway cannot move evidence away from its cause

The first root-cutaway litter layout translated leaves horizontally to fill the retained half. Seven of ten visible bodies then appeared over soil cells different from the authoritative recipients that received their pools. Cutaway now preserves ordinary matrices and hides an entire leaf if its body crosses the removed soil half; every shown leaf remains over retained terrain and its real recipient.

The first attractive relief-sorted scatter also allowed pairwise horizontal footprint interleaving. Final bodies were separated by relief, but a later leaf's vertical settle could pass through earlier litter. The ten deterministic landing lanes are still asymmetric in radius and heading, but their voxel footprints are pairwise horizontally disjoint, so every intermediate falling frame remains clear while exact final matrices and colours still hand to litter on the next tick.

## The instrument is truthful before the target is met

Soil-surface caching, fixed-order structural routing and direct reuse of already accepted stable typed batches remove repeated producer work without changing output; the living and falling leaf batch still rebuilds whenever wind or fall moves it. An intermediate unsafe identity-reuse path measured `14.35 / 22.48 / 26.95 ms` at p50/p95/p99, but it was rejected after the mutation critic changed prior-frame matrices, colours and ordered keys after the next frame existed and showed that shared caller-mutable arrays could make a corrupt pair appear unchanged; a shared soil-chunk voxel counter-run exposed the same class. Exact batch/chunk integrity then measured `17.70 / 26.14 / 30.36 ms`, but a second critic proved that caller mutation of `projectionCache.tissue.attachedLeafBodies[0].records` could poison a tissue hit and that mutating prior static geometry after its full snapshot was accepted could still yield an empty delta. The final design retains the reusable projection cache only in a private WeakMap, lazily freezes its caller-visible ordinary graph behind true read-only Map facades, and stores exact private expectations for every static resource field and typed buffer as well as ordered instance keys, matrices, colours, layout, presentation, incarnation, revisions and chunk voxels. Construction validates `previousFrame` before reuse and delta construction validates both frames. The abscised-health dependency also enters the topology key, so the cached and cold `healthFraction: 0` controls now both fail with the exact same `no basal petiole source` diagnostic rather than taking different failure paths. Across five matched Node 24 Windows observations of 60 consecutive day-100 producer ticks and frame constructions, the safe medians are `19.81 / 30.82 / 38.90 ms`, regressing the pre-reuse `18.73 / 28.40 / 36.10 ms` observation by `5.8% / 8.5% / 7.8%`; correctness replaces the optimization claim. A dated manual same-workstation control, not reproduced by the automated timing test, retained every cache, resource and frame guard but always rebuilt accepted batches, measuring `23.44 / 32.50 / 40.53 ms`; that observation made identity reuse `15.5% / 5.2% / 4.0%` faster than the equally safe control, but it is not a standing machine-independent performance claim. These are producer-only observations before browser runtime acceptance, GPU work or compositor presentation, not FPS or an end-to-end frame measurement. The p95 tail still exceeds the `16.67 ms` 60 Hz producer-work budget. The automated performance test reports current-path percentiles and cache hits as observational evidence; it does not reproduce the dated safe-control comparison, while structural-reuse, cache/cold parity and mutation counter-runs keep workstation timing out of the correctness gate.

The growth, leaf lifecycle, biogeochemistry, render-snapshot assembly, tissue mask and soil test modules were split back under the repository's 500-line ratchet without changing their public behavior. The browser milestone helpers were likewise extracted so the milestone visual gate stays below the ratchet rather than accumulating another exception.

## A root cutaway needs a root subject, not a whole-tree subject

The prior cutaway kept the full accepted tree in its camera-fit envelope and added the tree diagnostics height to its minimum distance. That was internally consistent and visually useless: material and azimuth gates could still find a few root pixels while the accepted belowground structure remained tiny. The camera now selects every exact accepted root-batch voxel, then follows the accepted shared face-neighbour lattice through the basal seed/bud body until the first reachable wood and only two wood-cell layers farther. Before radicle emergence, the accepted root count remains zero, both shaft witnesses remain null and the accepted seed batch alone defines the fit; a pre-shoot radicle retains that connected seed context without requiring wood that does not exist. This is fit geometry only: the renderer still draws the complete accepted frame, and ordinary hero, side, overhead and litter fitting are unchanged. In the mature `1280 × 720` HUD-reserved unit fixture, coarse and aggregate fine-root authority shafts span `166.29 px` and `227.02 px`; both must remain above `120 px`, and the exact selected bounds must remain clear of the HUD.
