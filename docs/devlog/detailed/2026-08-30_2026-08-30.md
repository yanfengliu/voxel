# Devlog — 2026-08-30

The owner asked for a biologically and materially accurate tree case study, approved a voxel-realistic *Quercus robur*, and chose an early-flush slice before recurrent-season, five-year and twenty-year checkpoints. The result stays a consumer fixture: Voxel receives versioned plain organ, soil and presentation data, while growth, resource exchange, phenology and mechanics remain outside renderer/core authority.

## The first green tree was biologically structured and visually wrong

The initial tests accepted pixels that first-hand inspection rejected: one DoubleSide leaf side rendered almost black, the soil dominated the frame, roots were not legible, the HUD competed with the seedling, and per-internode direction noise made a continuous extension unit look like stacked diamond joints. Fixed hero, side and overhead views exposed different defects, so the shoot now holds one axis across each extension unit, camera directions reveal complementary leaf faces, living leaf backfaces remain material-consistent, and the cutaway retains a pale aggregate fine-root material against wet soil.

The repair did not make the aggregate fine-root cohort into a claimed root system. It remains one bounded uptake surrogate, and the camera/test evidence says only that its shaft is projected and contrasted—not that individual roots, root hairs or fungal hyphae are resolved.

## Finite geometry invalidated centerline topology

The first continuity checks compared organ centerlines and missed physical impossibilities: the shoot began inside the acorn, the radicle left a gap, a finite rectangular petiole penetrated its parent, and a leaf that cleared the parent still crossed a continuation sibling. Germination now uses explicit opposite acorn surface ports, each lateral organ recomputes a tangential port from the finite basal section and the entire node radial envelope under self-weight and breeze, and branch rise clears the subtending leaf.

The standing private continuous-analysis gate reconstructs the former smooth source geometry, transforms its instance matrices, checks exact parent ports, samples tapered swept volumes, and tests leaf triangles at days 13, 90 and 100 plus breeze. It found failures the earlier coincident-point gate could not represent; it remains a bounded sampled biological-analysis oracle rather than visible render content, a formal constructive-solid-geometry proof or a watertight vascular-junction proof.

## Leaf material became one shape instead of three stories

The first mechanics path treated a 70 mm blade plus petiole while rendering 70 mm total, assumed a circular 1 mm petiole unrelated to the mesh, and claimed turgor changed pose when mechanics never read it. The shared descriptor now gives each primary-axis leaf a 70 mm blade plus short petiole; the resource-paid expanding axillary leaf uses the same geometry and mechanics law at a smaller similarity scale, with proportional area and pools. All variants retain a finite tapered rectangular petiole and the exact basal weak-axis second moment used by the beam reduction. Relative water content changes petiole stiffness and pose; chlorophyll loss can make yellow overtake green, while senescence alone makes brown.

The raised midrib and terminal bud marker remain readability geometry only. Neither is counted as lamina area, beam section or biomass, and that honesty boundary is recorded in the purpose accountability table.

## A rain recovery of 0.00000000000024 MPa was not recovery

The original low-water path multiplied root uptake by a fixed `0.08`; a 0.4 L intervention raised soil storage, but treatment and no-pulse controls each absorbed 0.0452406 L over the next seven days. Their mobile plant water differed by about `8.9e-16` L, and a strict greater-than assertion accepted a `2.4e-13` MPa leaf-potential difference as causal recovery.

Low-water conductance now follows root-weighted relative extractable water between wilting and field capacity, raised to an explicit fixture exponent of 3.5. Against the same-seed day-107 no-pulse control, 0.4 L of commanded rain retains 0.387 L more soil water, adds 0.0130 L root uptake and 0.00102 L plant water, improves mean leaf potential by 0.271 MPa, and adds `5.59e-6` kg assimilated carbon. The gate requires material floors below each measurement, exact source difference, ledger residuals below `1e-12`, and a no-root-uptake control in which rain wets soil without changing plant water or leaf potential.

Litter received the same scope correction. Its ablation proves direct ammonium, labile-phosphorus and respiration effects in soil; this short slice does not claim a separately identifiable whole-tree growth response from that pathway.

Ambient rain has its own honesty control. Removing all scheduled rain over the accepted 100-day ambient horizon materially changes boundary input and soil water, but the initially buffered soil leaves root uptake, plant water, leaf potential and assimilation indistinguishable at the test tolerance; only the paired low-water pulse supports a short-horizon tree-response claim.

## Three camera presets were not an inspectable camera

The host fits accepted public geometry while a preset owns the camera, then preserves the free pose across growth, wind, resize and capture without writing biological state. Hero, side, overhead, root cutaway, reset and a canvas double-click deliberately return to a fitted anchor. Browser evidence records the mode, anchor preset, orbit and centre, and the gate drives all three mouse buttons, the wheel and every WASD key before proving exact refit. First-hand review covered hero, side and overhead plus adversarial orbit, pan, zoom and bounded WASD frames; tree, soil, materials and HUD remained coherent at each retained view.

## Boundary and next work

The accepted case study ends after three early flushes and a terminal first-season litter-transfer check. It is not a mature tree, site predictor, full Farquhar solver, Richards equation, individual-root or fungal-network model, canopy light model, fracture simulation or recurrent annual cycle. Temperature and photoperiod, bud set and break, cambial growth, cohort turnover and held-out seedling measurements belong to the next checkpoint; five- and twenty-year claims wait for validated allometry, competition, mortality and disturbance evidence.
