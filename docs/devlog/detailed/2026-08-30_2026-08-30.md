# Devlog — 2026-08-30

The owner asked for a biologically and materially accurate tree case study, approved a voxel-realistic *Quercus robur*, and chose an early-flush slice before recurrent-season, five-year and twenty-year checkpoints. The result stays a consumer fixture: Voxel receives versioned plain organ, soil and presentation data, while growth, resource exchange, phenology and mechanics remain outside renderer/core authority.

## The first green tree was biologically structured and visually wrong

The initial tests accepted pixels that first-hand inspection rejected: one DoubleSide leaf side rendered almost black, the soil dominated the frame, roots were not legible, the HUD competed with the seedling, and per-internode direction noise made a continuous extension unit look like stacked diamond joints. Fixed hero, side and overhead views exposed different defects, so the shoot now holds one axis across each extension unit, camera directions reveal complementary leaf faces, living leaf backfaces remain material-consistent, and the cutaway retains a pale aggregate fine-root material against wet soil.

The repair did not make the aggregate fine-root cohort into a claimed root system. It remains one bounded uptake surrogate, and the camera/test evidence says only that its shaft is projected and contrasted—not that individual roots, root hairs or fungal hyphae are resolved.

## Finite geometry invalidated centerline topology

The first continuity checks compared organ centerlines and missed physical impossibilities: the shoot began inside the acorn, the radicle left a gap, a finite rectangular petiole penetrated its parent, and a leaf that cleared the parent still crossed a continuation sibling. Germination now uses explicit opposite acorn surface ports, each lateral organ recomputes a tangential port from the finite basal section and the entire node radial envelope under self-weight and breeze, and branch rise clears the subtending leaf.

The standing gate transforms the actual public geometry and instance matrices, checks exact parent ports, samples tapered swept volumes, and tests leaf triangles at days 13, 90 and 100 plus breeze. It found failures the earlier coincident-point gate could not represent; it remains a bounded sampled conflict oracle rather than a formal constructive-solid-geometry or watertight vascular-junction proof.

## Biomass and rendered wood had been different trees

The first authored radii made conserved fresh mass disagree with geometry-implied green mass by 2.65–11.07 times, with a representative branch at about 4.69 times. One shared allometry now solves the full dimensioned shaft against the exact regular-octagon area factor `2√2`, the shared taper selector feeds both geometry and accounting, and mechanics self-weight consumes the same fresh mass.

The first parity repair still measured a hypothetical full-length shaft while public projection shortened five shafts around pointed junction cones. The diagnostic reported 1.0 while owned mass divided by actual public-shaft mass ranged from 1.288 to 2.857. A second repair restored the full shaft but wrapped its end in a detached collar shell, duplicating material volume; preserving every collar ring in the conflict oracle exposed that the measurement was sharper while the object was still impossible. An occupied parent now has one four-ring shaft surface: its final 16% replaces the ordinary taper with a continuous flare, the complete enclosed volume enters the shared allometry solve, and no collar resource, batch or instance remains. The public gate groups every distinct Float32 ring and sums every transformed octagonal-frustum interval across all twelve day-100 dimensioned-wood organs, measuring owned/rendered mass ratios of 0.999999935–1.000000092. The result is still early-slice allometry and not a field-fitted diameter-growth law or watertight vascular-junction model.

## Leaf material became one shape instead of three stories

The first mechanics path treated a 70 mm blade plus petiole while rendering 70 mm total, assumed a circular 1 mm petiole unrelated to the mesh, and claimed turgor changed pose when mechanics never read it. The shared descriptor now gives each primary-axis leaf a 70 mm blade plus short petiole; the resource-paid expanding axillary leaf uses the same geometry and mechanics law at a smaller similarity scale, with proportional area and pools. All variants retain a finite tapered rectangular petiole and the exact basal weak-axis second moment used by the beam reduction. Relative water content changes petiole stiffness and pose; chlorophyll loss can make yellow overtake green, while senescence alone makes brown.

The raised midrib and terminal bud marker remain readability geometry only. Neither is counted as lamina area, beam section or biomass, and that honesty boundary is recorded in the purpose accountability table.

## Two measurements passed by omitting what they claimed

Sparse-delta parity initially compared only matrices and colours, so unchanged batch revisions could disagree while the test stayed green. Full snapshots now retain unchanged content revisions just as deltas do, and the parity check compares accepted metadata as well as content.

The adapter also called ten primary submissions a draw-call upper bound while the shadowed browser host measured nineteen actual draw calls. The metric is now named `primaryContentPassDrawCalls`, triangles carry the same scope, and both explicitly exclude shadow and auxiliary passes.

## A rain recovery of 0.00000000000024 MPa was not recovery

The original low-water path multiplied root uptake by a fixed `0.08`; a 0.4 L intervention raised soil storage, but treatment and no-pulse controls each absorbed 0.0452406 L over the next seven days. Their mobile plant water differed by about `8.9e-16` L, and a strict greater-than assertion accepted a `2.4e-13` MPa leaf-potential difference as causal recovery.

Low-water conductance now follows root-weighted relative extractable water between wilting and field capacity, raised to an explicit fixture exponent of 3.5. Against the same-seed day-107 no-pulse control, 0.4 L of commanded rain retains 0.387 L more soil water, adds 0.0130 L root uptake and 0.00102 L plant water, improves mean leaf potential by 0.271 MPa, and adds `5.59e-6` kg assimilated carbon. The gate requires material floors below each measurement, exact source difference, ledger residuals below `1e-12`, and a no-root-uptake control in which rain wets soil without changing plant water or leaf potential.

Litter received the same scope correction. Its ablation proves direct ammonium, labile-phosphorus and respiration effects in soil; this short slice does not claim a separately identifiable whole-tree growth response from that pathway.

Ambient rain has its own honesty control. Removing all scheduled rain over the accepted 100-day ambient horizon materially changes boundary input and soil water, but the initially buffered soil leaves root uptake, plant water, leaf potential and assimilation indistinguishable at the test tolerance; only the paired low-water pulse supports a short-horizon tree-response claim.

## Camera evidence now measures the render frame it names

The first camera instrument projected a synthetic box from centerline height and distal leaf radius, then returned that box as `subjectBoundsNdc`; visible blade width, camber, bud radius and pose were absent, and topology-only invalidation left the evidence stale. The fit now projects accepted public-geometry vertices from the current render frame, excludes hidden roots from ordinary views, includes them in cutaway, and uses those exact vertices for both distance and reported bounds.

The first live host advanced one simulation tick per `requestAnimationFrame`, so its stated one-biological-day-per-real-second mode would run four times too fast on the owner's 240 Hz display. A bounded timestamp accumulator now feeds the shared 60 Hz step; real-simulation counter-runs at 60, 120 and 240 Hz each land on exactly 86,400 biological seconds after one wall second, while pause discards elapsed time and explicit test transports remain deterministic.

## Three camera presets were not an inspectable camera

The owner reported the missing surface directly: the case study needed the same middle mouse, left click, right click and WASD contract as Scene and Model Studio. The canvas was not focusable and the host routed no pointer, wheel or held-key input. Its preset fitter also ran on every live biology presentation and resize, so a naïve orbit controller would have appeared to work for one event and then snapped back.

The host now shares Studio’s pure orbit, pan, zoom and keyboard movement laws and supplies only an oak-scale perspective adapter. Left-click takes canvas focus and never turns the view; middle-drag orbits after the matching four-pixel threshold; right-drag pans; wheel input zooms; held WASD moves on the camera-relative ground plane with diagonal normalization and a 50 ms frame cap. Blur, visibility loss and disposal clear held movement, pointer capture is released even if disposal interrupts a drag, controls retain keyboard ownership, and the visible hint states the contract rather than leaving it discoverable only by accident.

Preset and free navigation are separate presentation states. The host fits accepted public geometry while a preset owns the camera, then preserves the free pose across growth, wind, resize and capture without writing biological state. Hero, side, overhead, root cutaway, reset and a canvas double-click deliberately return to a fitted anchor. Browser evidence records the mode, anchor preset, orbit and centre, and the gate drives all three mouse buttons, the wheel and every WASD key before proving exact refit. First-hand review covered hero, side and overhead plus adversarial orbit, pan, zoom and bounded WASD frames; tree, soil, materials and HUD remained coherent at each retained view.

## The first remote oak gate exposed a measured timeout

Remote run 33334927705 was not green. Ubuntu Node 22 expired at the 45 s suite default in `oak-organ-conflicts.test.ts` while constructing the unrelated-valid-shaft counter-run; isolated local measurement put that exact test at 28.081 s, while its neighboring collision populations ranged from 0.019 s to 14.858 s. Only that test now declares `timeoutForMeasuredWorkMs(28_081)`, preserving the default for the rest and the repository’s slow-host allowance. The Windows Node 24 job also ended red, but its public annotation did not expose a test and authenticated logs were unavailable; the final local full gate is evidence for this change, not a claim about that unavailable remote failure.

## Boundary and next work

The accepted case study ends after three early flushes and a terminal first-season litter-transfer check. It is not a mature tree, site predictor, full Farquhar solver, Richards equation, individual-root or fungal-network model, canopy light model, fracture simulation or recurrent annual cycle. Temperature and photoperiod, bud set and break, cambial growth, cohort turnover and held-out seedling measurements belong to the next checkpoint; five- and twenty-year claims wait for validated allometry, competition, mortality and disturbance evidence.
