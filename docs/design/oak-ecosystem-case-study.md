# Pedunculate oak ecosystem case study

## Purpose and boundary

This case study asks whether a consumer can drive one biologically structured, materially legible tree through Voxel's existing public render contracts without turning the renderer into a simulation engine.

The consumer fixture in `fixtures/oak-ecosystem-consumer/` owns the organ graph, environmental state, process clocks, resource ledgers, interventions and wind pose.

The consumer adapter derives disposable profiled chunk content, cube-tissue instance batches, materials and sparse render deltas from the authoritative biology. Voxel receives only those versioned plain-data render records and never owns the organ state or process rules that produced them.

## Implemented slice

Run `npm run oak` from the repository root to start the fixture host at `http://127.0.0.1:5181/fixtures/oak-ecosystem-consumer/oak-browser-host.html` and open it in the default browser.

The implemented organism is a deterministic `Quercus robur` seedling from imbibition through three early shoot flushes, with a terminal first-season senescence and leaf-transfer path used as a conservation check.

This is an early-growth calibration hypothesis, not a field-calibrated tree-growth forecast.

The soil microcosm contains eight heterogeneous 0.2 m cells in two depth layers, with explicit water, ammonium, nitrate, labile phosphorus, sorbed phosphorus, litter and an aggregated ectomycorrhizal guild.

The outer host advances on the repository's shared 60 Hz step while physiology, soil chemistry, allocation and phenology run at their own explicit biological cadences. The browser maps real RAF timestamps through an eight-tick bounded accumulator, so 60, 120 and 240 Hz displays advance the same biological time and paused elapsed time is discarded rather than caught up.

The information panel reports recent successful RAF-driven canvas presentation cadence separately as `Render frame rate`. Every foreground RAF runtime attempt contributes elapsed time, only calls returning a presented manifest contribute a frame, and repeated unavailable attempts therefore decay to zero. That volatile display value excludes manual renders, resets across hidden-tab transitions and is neither the fixed simulation rate nor GPU-completion or compositor evidence.

Every organ has a stable local identity and generation, a parent edge, a biological stage, authoritative pose, geometry, tissue pools and stress state.

The shoot uses successive internodes, one leaf per node, oak's 2/5 spiral phyllotaxis and an axillary branch relationship; roots retain a taproot and a bounded spatial fine-root cohort rather than claiming resolved root topology.

## Cause-and-effect model

Rain enters topsoil, excess water runs off or drains, top cells evaporate, and roots can withdraw water or mineral nutrients only from cells touched by their declared spatial kernel; low-water root conductance is the root-weighted relative-extractable-water fraction raised to the registered `3.5` fixture exponent, so a paired intervention proves that a 0.4 L pulse leaves at least 0.30 L more soil water, adds at least 0.01 L root uptake and improves leaf water potential by at least 0.25 MPa rather than passing on floating-point noise.

Nitrogen retains separate ammonium and nitrate pools with nitrification, while phosphorus retains separate labile and sorbed pools with desorption.

The ectomycorrhizal guild receives plant carbon only in root-supported cells and can return nitrogen and phosphorus; a litter ablation proves decomposition directly raises soil ammonium and labile phosphorus while exporting respired carbon, without claiming that this short run produces a separately identifiable whole-tree growth response.

A bounded light-water-nutrient response produces carbon during the daylight interval, maintenance respiration and transpiration remove carbon and water, and stress changes leaf water potential, relative water content and chlorophyll.

Growth can occur only when carbon, water, nitrogen and phosphorus costs can all be paid, and each of the four whole-system ledgers compares initial storage plus boundary sources minus boundary sinks against current storage with an absolute residual below `1e-12` in the accepted scenarios.

Wind is a quasi-static cantilever response driven at the host tick, with a fixture-assumed `3–6 m/s` inspection breeze, dimensioned drag, tapered-section bending, self-weight from the biology-owned fresh mass, turgor-sensitive leaf-petiole stiffness and broad-leaf load reconfiguration; it does not simulate distal crown load, dynamic damping, fracture or thigmomorphogenesis.

## Material and rendering design

The visible scene is an explicitly hybrid voxel projection. One profiled `40 × 40 × 40` Voxel chunk addresses the `0.4 m` soil domain at the exact coarse pitch `655 / 65,536 m` (about `9.995 mm`). A shared deterministic surface oracle gives its columns localized multi-hummock-and-swale top-boundary levels at `-4`, `-3`, `-2`, `-1` and `0` coarse cells, retains an exact `6 × 6` level root-collar plateau and fills exactly four macrovoxels, about `39.98 mm`, beneath every local top. The ascending levels contain `158`, `398`, `568`, `356` and `120` of the `1,600` columns respectively, so the largest level owns `35.5%`; their connected-component populations are `65 / 57 / 36`, `238 / 160`, `540 / 13 / 9 / 6`, `152 / 110 / 94` and `58 / 36 / 26`. The full surface has about `39.98 mm` peak-to-trough relief, adjacent columns step by at most one level, no column is an isolated spike or pit and the longest straight same-level run is `17` cells. Root cutaway keeps the declared horizontal half and adds one aligned macrovoxel-thick local-height state wall through the remaining depth. The chunk is meshed and presented by Voxel's worker lane. The tree's above- and belowground source shapes are sampled and fused on one exact axis-aligned tissue lattice with pitch `131 / 65,536 m` (about `1.999 mm`). Both grids use half-cell-aligned centres, so every coarse soil macrovoxel is exactly `5 × 5 × 5` fine cells and their boundary faces coincide. Four living-tissue material-role batches form the connected plant union; fallen-litter and soil-contact batches remain outside it, while a seventh representative-weather batch remains outside material state. All seven instance the same cube geometry, and the contact batch reuses the soil material. Integer lattice translations and cube scale remain exact after Float32 serialization throughout the fixture's bounded coordinate range, so face-neighbour cubes meet without an epsilon.

Biology remains continuous and authoritative. Organ identity, parentage, length, radius, leaf area, taper, node-flare envelope, petiole section, tissue pools, stage and wind pose remain simulation or mechanics data; the adapter rasterizes those accepted shapes into disposable cubes after the biological step and never feeds cube occupancy or count back into growth, uptake, allocation or mechanics.

The continuous wood source retains its biology-owned fresh-mass and taper relationship, including the occupied-parent node-flare envelope, but the browser renders only the cube cover of that source rather than its former regular-octagon surfaces. The three deterministic lobed and cambered leaf source shapes, finite petiole and readability midrib likewise determine which tissue cubes are occupied; primary-axis leaves still own a 70 mm blade plus short petiole and the paid axillary leaf remains similarity-scaled smaller.

Every biological source contribution is assigned once to a unique material cell by a deterministic bounded free-cell search. Declared parent and child ports are reserved before deterministic bounded repair cells connect them to their source masks. A parent path may fuse only a direct child's source from that child's local axial layer 0 or 1, and the cell retains the child's source key and claim; this is declared proximal node fusion, not permission to re-own an unrelated or distal source. Public tissue keys name current material owner and world-lattice membership rather than promising an immutable organ-local display mask. The day-100 ordinary breeze-tick gate requires more than `97%` of membership to remain while also requiring some material to move.

The two display pitches are exact nested presentation scales rather than independent physical discretizations. Their shared half-cell alignment makes one coarse soil macrovoxel exactly five tissue cells along each edge, but neither grid changes the biology-owned process domain. A `131 / 65,536 m` tissue cube is a presentation sample, not a plant cell, anatomical volume, biomass parcel or claim that a real oak resolves at that scale. A `655 / 65,536 m` soil macrovoxel visualizes part of one biology-owned `0.2 m` process cell; it is not an individual pore, aggregate or soil ped, and the eight process cells remain the authority for water and nutrient exchange. Terrain relief is likewise static disposable display geometry rather than erosion, settlement or a ninth soil process cell. Exposed topsoil compares owning-cell water with a world-stable low-frequency two-dimensional warped monotone rank; at the two gated water baselines, small increases produce nested, non-isolated damp additions. Buried strata and cutaway walls retain a world-stable three-dimensional trilinearly interpolated field. Neither field is local hydrology. The deterministic palette makes water the dominant dark-versus-dry signal and keeps nitrogen and phosphorus accents subordinate, while litter colouring is eligible only on the actual local top boundary and never down the exposed cutaway wall.

The final tissue union is built before soil presentation. Every candidate coarse soil macrovoxel that would intersect final tissue or the continuous acorn source is removed from the worker-meshed chunk, then its volume is refilled with exact fine soil-contact cubes everywhere except living-tissue cells and the continuous acorn volume; touching faces remain legal. This preserves soil contact around roots and seed without leaving an air trench. The prior day-100 projection audit reported `131` root cubes with positive-volume soil intersection events, not `131` unique soil voxels. The current public-frame clearance sweep proves no positive-volume soil/tissue intersection at days 0, 3, 6, 13, 42, 82, 100, 210, 220, 239 and 240 in both surface and cutaway presentations.

Root voxel instances are absent from ordinary views and appear only in cutaway mode, where the coarse-root cube path is warm ochre-brown and the aggregate fine-root cohort path is pale. The latter remains one presentation-only cohort glyph rather than a measured individual-root diameter, count or resolved topology.

Living leaf colour comes from chlorophyll, phenological stage and resource stress, including a low-chlorophyll control in which yellow overtakes green, while authoritative leaf direction, turgor wilt and roll come from the simulation rather than a renderer animation. During senescence, an elapsed-season chlorophyll ceiling is applied after physiology so a later physiology step cannot visually restore an older leaf; day 210, 220 and 239 checks require monotonically advancing public voxel colour before day-240 abscission.

At abscission, the authoritative pools move into the declared recipient soil cell and living leaf masks disappear. A separate disposable presentation lays one flattened russet lobed mask per abscised leaf in deterministic canopy-biased clusters on same-height local terraces of the half-cell-aligned fine lattice. Every cube bottom face meets the retained local soil top; root cutaway deterministically repacks all ten complete masks inside its kept half rather than leaving glyphs over discarded soil. A lazy nearest-anchor traversal preserves the exact distance and tie ordering without materializing and sorting the full surface once per leaf, while deterministic packing still forbids duplicate litter cells or positive-volume contact with living tissue. The nonempty day-240 litter batch makes that transfer visible rather than leaving it only in a ledger. The masks preserve transferred leaf identity and form, but their position and quarter-turn are an inspection layout rather than a simulated fall trajectory, measured resting pose, decomposition geometry or second spatial pool.

The private browser host supplies the camera, sky fill, directional sun and shadow policy; none of those choices expand Voxel's public renderer API.

Hero, side, overhead and root-cutaway views fit the accepted public-geometry vertices rather than biological centerline proxies. Fit content includes exact transformed cube vertices from the accepted fallen-litter batch as well as active aboveground organs, while ordinary views still exclude hidden roots and transient weather cues so rain or wind cannot move the inspection camera. The hero elevation is high enough to expose lobed lamina area while retaining trunk height and soil context. The interactive host exposes pause, accelerated growth, wind inspection, rainfall, independent low-water, low-nitrogen and low-phosphorus regimes, reset and live ledger diagnostics. Its above-fold environmental readout keeps rain cue phase and liters, airflow and rain voxel counts, authoritative wind speed, topsoil water, mineral nitrogen and labile phosphorus visible without scrolling; the remaining live values stay in the lower HUD.

Weather presentation is bounded evidence, not a second simulation. The rain command starts thirty-six six-cube trails that descend for `78` host ticks while authoritative water remains unchanged; an active breeze advects them in the normalized direction shared with organ mechanics. Every cube bottom remains strictly airborne through the final falling frame. The first exact retained-terrain contact on the following impact frame triggers the `0.40 L` pulse for the consumer soil process, keeps one exact contact cube per surviving site, then emits varied bounded subsets of short outward and upward rebound trails for `24` ticks before disappearing. Wind mode sums the authoritative `3–6 m/s` sample at every shared `60 Hz` host tick and advances eighteen short periodic airflow packets along that same mechanics direction; it never substitutes elapsed ticks multiplied by the ending gust speed. Weather voxels avoid living tissue and occupy one transparent unlit batch; their opacity is material-owned so instance colours remain compatible with Voxel's batch contract. A paused host freezes both rain cue and the not-yet-contacted pulse. These cues do not resolve physical drops, drop speed, interception, puddles, turbulence or a second water or wind field.

Submitting a snapshot is not treated as presentation. Initial controls remain disabled until the first worker target is presented. After that first frame, one command that crosses a newer pending target is retained; the visible controls then disable until that intent is applied after runtime evidence reports the exact preceding render revision as presented. A direct harness call beyond the one bounded slot is rejected explicitly rather than dropped or allowed to grow an unbounded backlog. Biological time remains withheld while a target is pending. This prevents a later biological delta or a ready-then-click race from advancing against a chunk revision the runtime does not yet hold.

The inspection surface follows Studio’s one-pointer contract: left-click owns focus and never orbits, middle-drag turns, right-drag pans, wheel input zooms, and held WASD moves camera-relative across the ground plane. Those inputs change disposable presentation state only. A free camera survives live biology updates and viewport resize until a view button, root cutaway, reset or canvas double-click deliberately refits the accepted public geometry. Root cutaway uses its own measured hero azimuth so the declared aggregate fine-root path remains wider than three screen pixels instead of collapsing into the ordinary leaf-favouring view direction.

## Deliberate non-claims and next checkpoints

This slice is not a full Farquhar-von Caemmerer-Berry solver, Richards equation solver, individual-root or fungal-network model, canopy radiative-transfer model, field-calibrated wood allometry, watertight vascular-junction model, dynamic fracture model, species interaction network, mature-tree model or validated predictor for a particular site.

The next checkpoint is a calibrated recurrent annual cycle with temperature and photoperiod forcing, bud set and bud break, cohort turnover, cambial growth and held-out seedling measurements.

The later five- and twenty-year checkpoints require validated allometry, crown competition, root turnover, mortality and disturbance evidence before their outputs can be called biological predictions.
