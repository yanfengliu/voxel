# Pedunculate oak ecosystem case study

## Purpose and boundary

This case study asks whether a consumer can drive one biologically structured, materially legible tree through Voxel's existing public render contracts without turning the renderer into a simulation engine.

The consumer fixture in `fixtures/oak-ecosystem-consumer/` owns the organ graph, environmental state, process clocks, resource ledgers, interventions and wind pose.

The consumer adapter derives disposable profiled chunk content, cube-tissue instance batches, materials and sparse render deltas from the authoritative biology. Voxel receives only those versioned plain-data render records and never owns the organ state or process rules that produced them.

## Implemented slice

Run `npm run oak` from the repository root to start the fixture host at `http://127.0.0.1:5181/fixtures/oak-ecosystem-consumer/oak-browser-host.html` and open it in the default browser.

The implemented organism is a deterministic `Quercus robur` seedling from imbibition through three early shoot flushes, with a terminal first-season senescence and leaf-transfer path used as a conservation check.

This is an early-growth calibration hypothesis, not a field-calibrated tree-growth forecast.

The soil microcosm contains eight heterogeneous 0.2 m cells in two depth layers, with explicit water, ammonium, nitrate, labile phosphorus, sorbed phosphorus, litter and an aggregated ectomycorrhizal guild.

The outer host advances on the repository's shared 60 Hz step while physiology, soil chemistry, allocation and phenology run at their own explicit biological cadences. The browser maps real RAF timestamps through an eight-tick bounded accumulator, so 60, 120 and 240 Hz displays advance the same biological time and paused elapsed time is discarded rather than caught up.

The information panel reports recent successful RAF-driven canvas presentation cadence separately as `Render frame rate`. Every foreground RAF runtime attempt contributes elapsed time, only calls returning a presented manifest contribute a frame, and repeated unavailable attempts therefore decay to zero. That volatile display value excludes manual renders, resets across hidden-tab transitions and is neither the fixed simulation rate nor GPU-completion or compositor evidence.

Every organ has a stable local identity and generation, a parent edge, a biological stage, authoritative pose, current geometry, current tissue pools and stress state. Each active primary-development plan separately owns a stable target length, target radius, target resource pools and, for a leaf, target area; snapshots publish those targets as read-only authority beside the resource-paid current values, so a target never means that its tissue already exists.

The shoot uses successive internodes, one leaf per node, oak's 2/5 spiral phyllotaxis and an axillary branch relationship; roots retain a taproot and a bounded spatial fine-root cohort rather than claiming resolved root topology.

## Primary development and lifecycle

Flush authoring pays only the initial `0.005` development fraction rather than granting a complete organ. Every `900 s` allocation step advances a scheduled primary-development cap by at most `0.0025`, scales the requested increment by the available carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water plus the allocation carbon budget, debits those resources, and only then changes current dimensions. Structural axes use the same fraction for current length and radius, so their planned volume and target-pool fraction advance as its cube; leaf area advances linearly while leaf length and radius use its square root, and buds use its cube root. The `0.5 mm` minimum dimensioned-wood length keeps a paid primordium geometrically defined without granting its target radius or target pools. Carbon paid during preformation, division, expansion, maturation and maturity is retained in separate diagnostic cohorts whose sum is checked against current organ structural carbon.

The fixture uses bud swelling, cell division, cell expansion and maturation as explicit phases, but their numeric durations are calibration hypotheses. A flush first swells and consumes its old terminal bud, while newly authored organ identities remain `preformed`. After that gate clears, each child is exposed only when its already exposed parent is long enough to provide physical node clearance: a lateral leaf requires `max(2 mm, 1.5 × parent radius)`, while a distal axis, branch or bud requires `max(4 mm, 2.5 × parent radius)`. This lets a lateral leaf emerge before the downstream internode without placing the hidden continuation at the same node. Preformed identities remain in authoritative snapshots but are excluded from placed-shape conflict checks, mechanics, atmospheric leaf exchange and render projection until their parent chain activates them.

Detachment is likewise a sequence rather than an immediate litter teleport. Senescence begins at day 210; each leaf linearly resorbs `50%` of its starting structural nitrogen and `52%` of its starting structural phosphorus into the plant mobile reserve over its own interval from senescence onset to its staggered fracture, rather than reaching the target early and waiting. Leaves then sever their parent edges one at a time from day 240 through day 247.2 at `0.8 day` intervals, enter the `falling` phase and leave attached-canopy diagnostics while retaining identity, carbon, water and the unresorbed nutrient pools. A one-micrometre fracture cleft keeps the recolored existing parent wound record and falling Float32 body disjoint without a visible or display-sample-sized jump. Each authoritative pose advances for the fixture's `1.5 day` fall interval; only soil contact moves the remaining carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water into that leaf's declared soil recipient, zeros the organ pools and marks the leaf `abscised`. Day 240 captures the first detachment, day 244 is a mixed attached, falling and litter state, and day 249 is the first retained all-litter milestone.

## Cause-and-effect model

Rain enters topsoil, excess water runs off or drains, top cells evaporate, and roots can withdraw water or mineral nutrients only from cells touched by their declared spatial kernel; low-water root conductance is the root-weighted relative-extractable-water fraction raised to the registered `3.5` fixture exponent, so a paired intervention proves that a 0.4 L pulse leaves at least 0.30 L more soil water, adds at least 0.01 L root uptake and improves leaf water potential by at least 0.25 MPa rather than passing on floating-point noise.

Nitrogen retains separate ammonium and nitrate pools with nitrification, while phosphorus retains separate labile and sorbed pools with desorption.

The ectomycorrhizal guild receives plant carbon only in root-supported cells and can return nitrogen and phosphorus; a litter ablation proves decomposition directly raises soil ammonium and labile phosphorus while exporting respired carbon, without claiming that this short run produces a separately identifiable whole-tree growth response.

A bounded light-water-nutrient response produces carbon during the daylight interval, maintenance respiration and transpiration remove carbon and water, and stress changes leaf water potential, relative water content and chlorophyll.

Growth can occur only when carbon, water, nitrogen and phosphorus costs can all be paid; scheduled time limits what may be requested but never substitutes for payment. Each of the four whole-system ledgers compares initial storage plus boundary sources minus boundary sinks against current storage with an absolute residual below `1e-12` in the accepted scenarios.

The authored first-year topology is deliberately finite. Once all three flushes have completed and no funded primary-development sink remains, organ dimensions, structural pools and tissue topology stay fixed. Photosynthetic carbon above the declared mobile reserve leaves through the named `post-primary-carbon-overflow` boundary sink and its own diagnostic counter; a no-overflow counter-run stockpiles that same excess. This export is not respiration and does not stand in for cambial thickening, storage-organ growth or a recurrent annual cycle, none of which this slice implements.

Wind is a quasi-static cantilever response driven at the host tick, with a fixture-assumed `3–6 m/s` inspection breeze, dimensioned drag, piecewise-cylindrical section bending, self-weight from the biology-owned fresh mass, turgor-sensitive leaf-petiole stiffness and broad-leaf load reconfiguration; it does not simulate distal crown load, dynamic damping, fracture or thigmomorphogenesis.

## Deliberate non-claims and next checkpoints
